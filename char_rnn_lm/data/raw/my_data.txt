Story: The Honest Woodcutter

A poor woodcutter lived in a small village. One day, while cutting wood by the river, his axe slipped and fell into the water. He grew sad and began to weep. Suddenly a water fairy rose from the river, held up a golden axe and asked, "Is this yours?" The woodcutter said, "No, mine was made of iron."

The fairy was delighted by his honesty. She returned his iron axe and, as a reward, gave him the golden and silver axes too.

Moral: Honesty is always rewarded.
